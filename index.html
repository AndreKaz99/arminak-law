The Firm

A Glendale litigation boutique that pairs the rigor of a large firm with the attention of a private counsel — trusted by families, executives and Fortune 500 companies alike.

From catastrophic personal injury and wrongful death to high-stakes business and employment disputes, Arminak Law represents clients across Glendale, Burbank, Pasadena and greater Los Angeles in state and federal court.

Counsel for the matters that define lives and livelihoods.
