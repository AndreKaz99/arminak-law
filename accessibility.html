Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Arminak Law, APC is committed to ensuring that everyone, including people with disabilities, can use our website. We design and test this site to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA, the standard referenced under the Americans with Disabilities Act.

What we do

  • Semantic page structure with landmarks, a logical heading order and a skip link to the main content.
  • Text, form fields and interface controls that meet WCAG AA contrast ratios.
  • Full keyboard access with visible focus indicators that are never hidden behind the sticky header.
  • A Pause motion control beside each scrolling strip, which pauses all automatic movement on the site and is remembered on your next visit.
  • Animations, smooth scrolling and parallax effects that switch off when your device requests reduced motion.
  • Animated figures that are announced to screen readers as their final values.
  • Form fields with persistent labels, clearly marked optional fields, and error messages linked to each field.
  • Descriptive alternative text for meaningful images, and links that announce when they open a new tab.
  • Layouts that reflow without horizontal scrolling down to 320 pixels wide and support text zoom to 200%.
