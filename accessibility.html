Accessibility

Accessibility statement

Arminak Law, APC is committed to ensuring that everyone, including people with disabilities, can use our website. We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we do

  • Semantic page structure with landmarks, headings and a skip link.
  • Text and interface colours that meet WCAG AA contrast ratios.
  • Full keyboard navigation with visible focus indicators.
  • Animations that are disabled when your device requests reduced motion.
  • Form fields with persistent labels and announced error messages.
